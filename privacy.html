Privacy

We collect the minimum needed to run the service.

What we keep

  • Your email address.
  • The traditions you chose, your time zone and your send time.
  • Your WhatsApp number and the time you agreed to WhatsApp messages, only if you add them.
  • Your mobile number, only if you choose to give it. It is optional and used only for WhatsApp messages.
  • If you choose Telegram, the chat ID Telegram gives our bot when you press Start. We never see your phone number or name from Telegram.
  • A record of which days we sent you a message, so we never send twice in a day.
  • Anything you write on the feedback form, and your name and email if you choose to give them.

Healthy Living

If you use Healthy Living, we store the goals and time you chose, the habits in your plan, and which habits you ticked each day. This is general wellness information. We don't ask about medical conditions. It is linked to a random code kept on your device, not to your name or email. You can delete all of it with “Delete my data” on the Healthy Living page.

Mindful Moments

If you save a session, we store the day, the type (breathing or meditation), the minutes, and the mood and note if you add them. It is linked to the same random code as Healthy Living, not to your name or email. You can delete single entries from your journal, or everything with “Delete my data” on the Healthy Living page.

Sun and Moon, and your calendar

The sunrise, sunset and moon times are worked out inside your browser. The place you choose, or your location if you tap “Use my location”, is kept only on your device and never sent to us. The calendar feed contains only the festivals for the traditions in its link. It has no personal information.

Visit statistics

We count page views to see what is useful. We don't use cookies and we don't store your IP address. Each visit is stored with a short anonymous code that changes every day, the page, the website you came from, whether you used a phone or computer, and your time zone. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, we don't count your visit.

Who processes it

We use Supabase to store data, Resend to deliver email, Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to deliver WhatsApp messages, and Telegram to deliver Telegram messages. They process your details only to provide those services. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from email with the link in any email.
  • Stop WhatsApp by replying STOP, or remove your number on the preferences page.
  • Stop Telegram by sending /stop to the bot, or untick Telegram on the preferences page.
  • Change your traditions or time on the preferences page, linked in every email.

After you unsubscribe we keep your email address and a note that you unsubscribed so we don't message you again. To have your data deleted completely, send a message on the feedback page or use the contact details in the footer.

WhatsApp consent

We only message a number after you tick the consent box and verify the number with a code sent to it. Messages are one short daily note with a link.
