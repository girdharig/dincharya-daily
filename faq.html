Questions

Is Dincharya free?

Yes. The daily email and the WhatsApp message are free to receive.

What do I get each day?

Today's festivals, fasts, rituals and a small practice for the traditions you choose, plus a short story. On WhatsApp it is a single short message with a link to the full details.

How do I subscribe?

Open the Subscribe tab, choose your traditions and send time, and enter your email. Tap the confirmation link we email you. You can add your WhatsApp number on the same form.

How does WhatsApp work?

Add your number with country code and tick the consent box. After you confirm your email we send a 6-digit code on WhatsApp to prove the number is yours. Once verified, you get one short message a day at your chosen time, with a link to the app.

How does Telegram work?

Tick Telegram when you subscribe. After you confirm your email, tap the button that opens our Telegram bot and press Start. That connects your chat, and you'll get the daily message there at your chosen time. Send /stop to the bot to pause and /start to resume. Telegram doesn't need your phone number.

Do I have to give my mobile number?

No. It's optional. We only use it if you choose WhatsApp messages. Email and Telegram don't need it.

How do I add the festivals to my phone's calendar?

Open the Calendar tab and tap "Google Calendar" or "Apple / Outlook" in the "Add these to your calendar" box. It adds a live calendar for your traditions that updates by itself. Lunar festival dates can differ by region.

How accurate are the sunrise, sunset and moon times?

They are calculated for the place you choose, usually within a minute or two of published tables. Your community's own timetable can differ by a few minutes, so follow it for fasts and prayers.

Can I make the text bigger or switch to dark mode?

Yes. Tap "Aa" at the top of any page to choose Normal, Large or Extra large text, and an Auto, Light or Dark theme.

How do I stop WhatsApp messages?

Reply STOP to any message, or remove your number from the preferences page linked in every email.

How do I stop the emails?

Every email has an Unsubscribe link. One tap and it's done.

Can I follow more than one tradition?

Yes. Pick as many as you like. Your message lists each one.

What time will I get my message?

The time you choose, in your own time zone.

Are the dates exact?

We use the most widely observed date. Lunar festivals can fall on different days in different communities or regions, and Islamic dates depend on moon sighting. Follow your community's calendar if it differs.

I found a mistake or something isn't working.

Thank you for telling us. Use the feedback page to report an issue, a wrong date, or a complaint. We read every message.

Who sees my details?

Only the services that deliver your messages. We don't sell your data. See the privacy page.

Which languages?

English for now. More are planned. Tell us which you need.
