How Dincharya helps

Never miss a festival or fast

Dates arrive before the day, so you can plan the lamp, the meal, the leave from work, or the call to family.

A small daily practice

One short prayer, meditation, act of service or reflection. Two minutes, with no app to open if you don't want to.

Stories to share

A short story from your tradition each day. Read it yourself, or tell it to a child at dinner.

Know why it matters

Every festival and fast comes with a short note on its meaning, and every story ends with what it teaches.

Follow more than one tradition

Many families and friendships cross traditions. Pick several and see what the people you love are observing today.

Calm, not noisy
